Model Weaknesses Identified & Next Steps

1. K Score Undersells Recent-Breakout Pitchers

Evidence: Jump (11K) and Cole (9K) were both rated "Manageable" (48–50 range). Their K scores reflect season-long K%, not recent hot streaks. A pitcher who's struck out 9+ in 3 of his last 5 starts should get a multiplier.

Fix to add: recent_k_surge flag — when avg_k_per_9 over last 3 starts > 1.5× season K/9, add +8–12 to k_score. This would have caught both Jump and the correct deGrom output.

2. Walk Prop False Positives (Chandler, Alvarez)

Evidence: Chandler (69.0 WALK MACHINE) had only 1 BB in 6 clean innings. Alvarez (77.2 WALK MACHINE) had 1 BB in 5.1 IP. Both were top-2 walk scores yet both pitched efficiently. Walk score is built on career BB% which can include old injury seasons or early-career wildness that doesn't reflect current command.

Fix to add: weight recent 30-day BB% at 60% (currently season-long). Chandler's recent control work is better than his career average suggests. A "trending control" flag — when trailing 30-day F-strike% is 5%+ better than career average — would suppress the WALK MACHINE label.

3. Fantasy Engine Integration (FIXED today)

Gap: hitter_fantasy_engine has been running daily since 7/31 but its power_score, contact_score, obp_score, proj_hits, proj_dk were not surfaced in any output. This left hit props, DFS lineup value, and batter-side walk confirmations completely invisible.

What's fixed: gen_hr_board.py now shows PwrScore, projH, and DK on each player card. Slate writeup now has dedicated Hit Props and Batter OBP sections. Dual-confirmation walk plays (pitcher walk score + batter OBP score) are now identified explicitly.

4. HR Props Need Batter-Side Confirmation

Evidence: Schwarber missed yesterday despite correct pitcher vulnerability and park factor. The HR engine ranks batters but doesn't use fantasy power_score as a cross-check. Today power_score is now displayed alongside hr_score — when both are 70+, it's the highest-confidence play.

Rule going forward: Best HR picks = hr_score ≥ 70 AND power_score ≥ 70 AND pitcher Neutral Lean or better AND park factor ≥ 1.0. Today: Schwarber qualifies (hr=80.7, pow=81.5, Bieber NL, CBP ×1.14).
